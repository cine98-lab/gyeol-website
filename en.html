Gyeol: the pattern
time leaves in wood

In Korean, gyeol is the grain of wood, the texture of skin, the temper of a heart. We don’t hide our materials, we finish every piece by hand, and we make furniture that deepens with use.

  • Material

    We name every species, where it grew and how it’s finished.

  • Craft

    Each piece carries the name of its maker and the date it was made.

  • Time

    Joinery guaranteed for life, and refinished whenever it needs it.

  • Proportion

    Sized for Korean living rooms and bedrooms.

The time of one tree

From a single tree to a family’s table, every piece passes through five unhurried stages.

  1. Selecting

    We choose timber with straight grain and sound knots.

  2. Drying

    The wood dries slowly until it settles.

  3. Joining

    Pieces lock into one another, with as little metal as possible.

  4. Finishing

    Hand-rubbed oil and wax keep the grain in view.

  5. Coming home

    We deliver and install every piece ourselves.
